ICEX × VivaTech 2026
Spanish Delegation · June 16–19, Paris

Spain meets the
Paris innovation ecosystem

On June 16–19 — around VivaTech 2026 — ICEX España brings an official delegation of 67 Spanish companies, startups, scaleups, and investors to Paris for a structured immersion into the French tech and deep-tech ecosystem. The delegation spans AI, deeptech, aerospace, defence, healthtech, cleantech, fintech, and strategic sovereignty technologies.

Every session is built around concrete outputs: qualified introductions to French corporates, co-investment conversations with French VC funds, and access to open innovation programmes. Facilitated by Impulse Partners, the programme closes with a joint networking cocktail bringing the full Spanish delegation together with a curated group of French counterparts.
